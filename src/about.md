I'm Edward. I'm curious about how the world fits together.

Mechanical or software engineering, construction, infrastructure and urban design all fascinate me: how the parts fit together, why something was built a certain way, and how it shapes everyday life.

I've travelled by train across America, Japan, Europe and Australia, and explored the South Pacific. I enjoy spending time in different places and getting a feel for daily life, the journey is the destination for me, whether by plane, train or ship, or wandering on foot or by bike.

There's always another place to explore, or something familiar to see anew.
